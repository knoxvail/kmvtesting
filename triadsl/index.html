Senior living management, consulting, and project turn-around since 1990

Since 1990, Triad Senior Living entities have developed, owned, or managed senior living communities across the country, providing senior living management, consulting, and project turn-around. Our group is large enough to have a deep bench of experienced professionals but small enough to react quickly and effectively to meet any management challenge related to senior living.

Management

Full-service management across independent living, assisted living, skilled nursing, and long-term care. We staff our communities with capable, experienced on-site leadership and give them the autonomy and flexibility to meet resident needs quickly and effectively.

Joint Ventures & Financing

Triad has extensive experience working with varied ownership groups and financing mechanisms. From joint ventures with public companies like Capital Senior Living, Inc. (now Sonida Senior Living) to third-party management for national non-profits like Affordable Community Housing Trust, Triad has added value in both operations and project strategy. We are also familiar with a variety of real estate financing utilizing FNMA, HUD and tax-exempt bonds. With over 30 years and over $300M in senior housing financing, Triad excels in helping determine the best avenue available for each specific and unique project opportunity.

Senior living management, consulting, and project turn-around services

Consulting

Operational assessments, financial restructuring support, and interim or receivership management for ownership groups, lenders, and courts navigating complex or financially distressed senior living assets.

North Dallas, TX

A Triad entity was appointed by the court as receiver for a 100-unit assisted living and memory care community. Over a 14-month engagement, Triad stabilized occupancy and staffing and managed all day-to-day operations, working with the lender and court toward an orderly transition to the long-term owner.

Southwest Florida

Triad was engaged by a national mutual fund company that had financed a 134-unit independent living community. Triad's on-site review, including a competitive market study and resident council review, informed the lender and was instrumental in the asset's ultimate sale to a new owner.

Project turn-around

Repositioning underperforming senior living communities: addressing occupancy challenges, operational inefficiencies, and market positioning, with marketing and sales strategy as a core part of the process.

Houston, TX

Triad was engaged by a lender group on an asset in default of its mortgage obligations amid an acrimonious lender-owner relationship. Over 10 months, Triad managed on-site operations and worked with both parties to a sale that retained community staff, avoided foreclosure, and achieved a smooth transition without additional litigation.
